# Support and Onboarding

Nimbus Cloud offers three support plans: Basic, Business and Enterprise.

## Response times

Basic support is provided by email only, with a first response target of 24 hours. Business support adds live chat and a first response target of 4 hours for high-severity tickets. Enterprise support includes a named technical account manager and a 15-minute first response target for critical incidents, available 24/7.

## Onboarding

Enterprise customers receive a guided onboarding programme that runs for 6 weeks, including an architecture review and a migration plan. Business customers can book two onboarding sessions with a solutions engineer.

## Languages

Support is available in English and Arabic, with Hindi available during India business hours.
