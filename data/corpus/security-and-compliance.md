# Security and Compliance

All customer data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher. Encryption keys are managed in a hardware security module and rotated every 90 days.

## Certifications

Nimbus Cloud holds SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certifications. Audit reports are available to customers under NDA from the trust portal.

## Access control

Production access for Nimbus engineers requires single sign-on, hardware security keys and a just-in-time approval that expires after 4 hours. Every production session is recorded and retained for one year.

## Vulnerability disclosure

Security researchers can report vulnerabilities to security@nimbus.example. We acknowledge reports within 2 business days and run a bug bounty programme with rewards of up to 10,000 USD for critical findings.
