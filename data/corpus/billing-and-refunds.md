# Billing and Refunds

Nimbus Cloud bills all accounts monthly in arrears, on the first business day of each month. Invoices are issued in USD by default; customers in the GCC and India can request invoicing in AED, SAR or INR from the billing console.

## Payment methods

We accept credit cards, ACH transfers and wire transfers. Enterprise customers on annual contracts may pay by purchase order with net-30 terms.

## Refund policy

Customers can request a full refund within 14 days of their first paid invoice. After the first 14 days, refunds are issued only as service credits, never as cash. Refund requests must be submitted through a billing support ticket and are processed within 5 business days.

## Late payments

If an invoice remains unpaid for 30 days, the account enters a grace period. After 45 days without payment, compute resources are suspended, but stored data is retained for an additional 30 days before deletion.
