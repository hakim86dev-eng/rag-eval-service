# Service Level Agreement

Nimbus Cloud commits to a monthly uptime of 99.95% for the managed Postgres and object storage services, and 99.9% for the serverless functions platform.

## Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below the committed level, customers are eligible for service credits. Uptime between 99.0% and the committed level earns a credit of 10% of the monthly bill for the affected service. Uptime below 99.0% earns a credit of 25%. Credits must be claimed within 30 days of the end of the affected month.

## Exclusions

The SLA does not cover scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance, outages caused by customer misconfiguration, or features labelled as beta.

## Incident communication

During an incident, status updates are posted to status.nimbus.example every 30 minutes until resolution. A written post-incident review is published within 5 business days for any incident lasting longer than one hour.
